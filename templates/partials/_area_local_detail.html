This exists because the fields it renders are the ones that have to be genuinely different per town — swap the town name into a page and if this block still reads the same, Google is looking at a doorway page. They come from AREA_PAGES in core/content.py and are policed by core.checks.area_pages_have_local_detail. `area_example_jobs` arrives with TODO placeholders already filtered out by the view, so the section simply does not render until real jobs are written. That is deliberate: an empty section is honest, an invented one is not. The reciprocal service links live in _area_service_links.html — that block is part of the internal-linking mesh and changes for different reasons than this. {% endcomment %}
