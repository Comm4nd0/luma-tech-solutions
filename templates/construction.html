Site security & ANPR

Who was on site, when — without the clipboard.

Sub-contractor billing disputes, RAMS compliance, H&S audits, insurance claims, out-of-hours gate breaches — all of it gets argued from memory and a half-filled-in paper log. Gate ANPR and a handful of well-placed cameras end the argument: timestamped footage and a searchable vehicle log, recorded on your kit in a lockable site cabinet.

The commercial model

  • Installed at site set-up — gate ANPR, yard and perimeter coverage, recorder in a lockable cabinet, 4G failover until your fixed line goes in.
  • One fixed monthly price for the duration — kit, monitoring config and support in a single figure your QS can put in the prelims. No per-camera surprises.
  • End of programme, your choice — we decommission, or we move the system straight to your next compound. Multi-site builders run the same setup site after site under one arrangement.

What the site manager gets

  • A daily presence report — vehicles on site and when, auto-emailed at the end of the working day.
  • Searchable history — every visit by plate, with clips and timestamps, in seconds.
  • Out-of-hours alerts only — quiet during the working day, a buzz for movement at the gate after the site closes.
  • One phone dashboard — iOS and Android, two-factor, no public ports opened.

The legal bit, handled

Workplace ANPR is personal-data processing, which means ICO registration, a Data Protection Impact Assessment, compliant signage and a retention policy. We draft the DPIA, supply the signs, and hand over a one-page "what we record, how long for, who can see it" sheet for your H&S file — it's part of the install package, not an extra.

New-build pre-wire

Run it before the plasterboard — or pay three times as much after.

Buyers of a new home in Marlow, Henley or Maidenhead expect Wi-Fi that works in every room, CCTV that doesn't trail cables down the render, and a house that's ready for whatever they plug in next. The only cheap moment to make that true is first fix. We design and install the low-voltage layer so your electrician can get on with their own package.

How it runs on your programme

  • Design from your drawings — AP, camera, door-entry and data positions marked up per house type, with containment requirements issued to your electrician before first fix.
  • First fix — Cat6/Cat6a pulled to every position and back to a comms enclosure, alongside the sparky's runs, before plasterboard. We work to your programme, not ours.
  • Second fix & commissioning — faceplates, patching, testing and labelling at decoration stage; access points, CCTV and gateway commissioned before completion if the plot is sold with them.
  • Handover per plot — test results, as-built diagram and a plain-English pack the purchaser (or their own IT contractor) can pick up cold. A better spec sheet for your sales office, too.

Why bother, commercially

  • A differentiator that costs hundreds, not thousands — "wired for whole-home Wi-Fi and CCTV" is a line on the sales particulars that the volume builders down the road don't have.
  • No retrofit call-backs — the number-one post-completion tech complaint in a larger new-build is Wi-Fi that doesn't reach. Pre-wire kills it before it exists.
  • One number per house type — quoted from drawings, fixed, repeatable across every plot of that type. Re-quoted only if the drawings change.
