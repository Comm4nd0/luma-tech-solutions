What we do for Maidenhead homes and businesses

Wi-Fi for larger and older properties

Maidenhead has more 200+ m² houses than most of the Thames Valley, and a fair number of older period builds with thick walls. The standard mesh-and-booster recipe doesn't cope. We design wired UniFi networks with multiple ceiling-mounted access points, sized from a thorough on-site survey, fed by Cat6 to a tidy comms cupboard. Typical Maidenhead installs run from five to eleven access points depending on size and shape. More about networking →

CCTV with the recorder in your house

UniFi Protect cameras with on-device person, vehicle and package detection, and (where useful) number-plate recognition on the drive. Recordings live on a small NVR at your house rather than in someone else's cloud — no monthly subscription and no third-party AI looking through your footage. Camera positions planned room-by-room and on-site so coverage is verified before anything is drilled. More about physical security →

Smart home that stays in your house

Home Assistant at the core — lighting, climate, scenes, and a local voice assistant if you want one. Runs on a small box at your house, works without the internet, no cloud lock-in. More about automation →

Ongoing care, by the same engineer

Optional care plans cover monitoring, firmware updates, quarterly health checks and a known response time. Same engineer takes the call every time — no tier-one call centre between you and someone who knows the system. More about support →
