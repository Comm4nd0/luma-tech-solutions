Wi-Fi, CCTV and IT support in Beaconsfield.

Beaconsfield has some of the largest detached homes in the Thames Valley — and a tight, old town centre full of period properties. Both are notoriously hard to cover with consumer mesh kit. We design expertly engineered UniFi networks that reach every room, every outbuilding, and the bottom of the garden.

Beaconsfield's challenge: big houses, old town centre

Beaconsfield's housing stock is two extremes glued together — the larger detached new-builds out toward Knotty Green and Forty Green that simply can't be covered by a single router, and the Georgian / Victorian period properties around the old town with thick walls, lath-and-plaster, and conservation constraints on what you can fix to the outside.

Both punish consumer mesh kit. The answer in both cases is the same: wired access points, sized from an actual site survey, fed by Cat6 to a tidy comms cupboard. One managed dashboard, real diagnostics, no rebooting the router.

What we do for Beaconsfield homes and businesses

Wi-Fi installation for larger properties

Properly engineered UniFi networks — multiple wired access points, sized from an on-site survey, fed by Cat6 to a tidy comms cupboard. One managed dashboard, no mesh, no boosters. Typical Beaconsfield installs run from four to ten access points depending on size, layout and whether the outbuilding or pool house needs coverage too. More about networking →

CCTV for period and listed properties

UniFi Protect cameras with on-device person, vehicle and package detection, recordings stored on a small NVR at your house — no monthly cloud subscription, footage stays on your kit. Mounts and cable runs planned to be discreet on period façades, so the old town centre look isn't ruined by a fishmonger-sign of cameras and boxes. More about physical security →

Smart home that works without the cloud

Home Assistant at the core — lighting, climate, security, scenes — running on a small box at your house. Local voice control if you want it; no Amazon or Google recordings going anywhere. Keeps working when the BT exchange takes a Sunday off. More about automation →

Ongoing care, by the same engineer

Optional care plans cover monitoring, firmware updates, quarterly health checks and a known response time. Same engineer takes the call every time — no tier-one call centre. More about support →
