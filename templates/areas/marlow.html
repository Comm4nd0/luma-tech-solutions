Wi-Fi, CCTV and IT support in Marlow.

We're based in Marlow. That means short drives, fast response, and an engineer who knows the housing stock — from the Edwardian and Georgian properties around the High Street to the larger detached houses up Marlow Bottom and Munday Dean.

Based in Marlow, on the doorstep

Most of our work happens within a twenty-minute drive of the town centre — Marlow itself, Bourne End, Little Marlow, Marlow Bottom, and across the bridge into Bisham. Being local matters for the things that consumer-kit installers and big national chains don't do well: walking the property thoroughly before quoting, turning up the same week when something stops working, and knowing the kind of building you're working in before you start drilling holes.

Marlow has a particular mix — period stock around the High Street and Quarry Wood that punishes consumer Wi-Fi mesh (thick walls, lath-and-plaster, foil-backed insulation), and larger modern detached houses up the hill that are simply too big for a single router to cover. We've engineered networks for both, and the answer is rarely "buy a bigger mesh kit".

What we do for Marlow homes and businesses

Wi-Fi that reaches every room

Every Marlow install starts with a walk round the property rather than a guess over the phone — we're ten minutes away, so there's no excuse for skipping it. What comes back is a wired UniFi design: ceiling access points where the survey says they need to be, Cat6 home to a tidy comms cupboard, one dashboard that shows what the network is actually doing. Typical Marlow projects run to between three and eleven access points depending on the size and shape of the property. No mesh, no boosters, no rebooting the router every Sunday morning. CCTV that records to your house

UniFi Protect cameras with on-device person/vehicle/package detection and the recorder living in your house — no monthly cloud subscription, footage stays on your kit. Camera positions are planned room-by-room and on-site so coverage is verified before anything is drilled. More about physical security →

Smart home that works without the internet

Home Assistant on a small box in your own house, so the lighting, heating and door locks answer to hardware you own rather than a subscription. Local voice control if you want it, with no Amazon or Google recordings leaving the property — and the scenes still fire on the mornings the broadband doesn't. More about automation →

Ongoing care, not hit-and-run

You get the same engineer every time you call: the one who designed the system and knows where the cables run.
