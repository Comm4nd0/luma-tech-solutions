Why Henley is harder than it looks

The town centre is full of period properties — Georgian townhouses, Victorian semis, listed riverside boathouses — where lath-and-plaster, thick stone, foil-backed insulation and cast-iron radiators conspire to absorb every bit of Wi-Fi signal a single router can put out. Push out toward Remenham, Hambleden, Aston or up the hill toward Bix and the houses get bigger and the floor plans more rambling. Very few of them are well served by the kit BT or Sky drops in the box.

We've engineered networks for both ends of that spectrum — from a four-bed Georgian on Hart Street to larger detacheds out by the regatta course — and the answer is rarely "buy a bigger mesh kit". It's wired access points, sized correctly, fed by Cat6, on one managed dashboard.
