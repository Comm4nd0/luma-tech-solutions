Access control

For businesses and shared sites we install commercial-grade access control — door readers, phone-based unlocks and tap-to-enter cards, all on the same dashboard as your network and CCTV.

  • Gate intercoms — video doorbells or commercial intercoms with two-way audio and remote unlock.
  • Visitor management — temporary access codes for tradespeople and Airbnb guests, with a photo at entry and a record of who came in when.
  • Time-based rules — the cleaner gets 8–10am Monday only, automatically.
  • Phone-based keys — your phone unlocks the door; fobs as backup. Revoke instantly when staff leave.

Where it earns its keep

  • Staff turnover — revoking a phone key takes seconds. Recovering a lost fob, or rekeying a lock, does not.
  • Shared and multi-tenant sites — each tenant, contractor and cleaner gets their own credential and their own audit trail.
  • Gated properties — remote unlock from your phone whether you are upstairs or abroad, with two-way audio at the gate.
  • Deliveries — a time-limited code and a photo at entry, instead of a key under a plant pot.

Integrated, not bolted on

Access control installed in isolation gives you a second app, a second login and a second set of logs that never quite line up with the cameras. We put entry events and camera footage on one system, so "who opened the back door at 6:40" is one search rather than a reconciliation exercise. The network segmentation that keeps the readers off your office LAN is part of the same design — see how we build the network.

How a project comes together

Every install is quoted from a site visit. We walk the doors and gates, work out what needs a reader and what needs an intercom, and write up a fixed-price proposal covering hardware, labour, cabling and timeline — no day rates, no scope creep.
