How we work

A consistent process across every service.

Discovery call

A 30-minute conversation — usually a video call, sometimes on-site. We listen to what you're trying to achieve, ask the awkward questions, and tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

Site survey & proposal

For networking and automation jobs we'll come and look at the property. You'll get a fixed-price proposal with the scope, hardware list and timeline written down — no day-rate surprises.

Engineering & install

We build it, we set it up, we test it, we document it. You walk away with a plain-English handover pack that covers every cable run, every login and every automation — so you (or anyone) can pick it up later.

Ongoing care

Optional care plans pick up where the install leaves off. Monitoring, updates, tweaks, advice. The same engineer every time you call.
