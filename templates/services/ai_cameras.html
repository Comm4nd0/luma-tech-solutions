For construction & site managers

ANPR & on-site presence, without a clipboard.

Build sites have a real, expensive problem: nobody actually knows who was on site, when, and for how long. Sub-contractor billing disputes, RAMS compliance, H&S audits, insurance claims, late-night gate breaches — all of it gets argued from memory and a half-filled-in paper log. AI cameras with on-board number- plate recognition fix it cleanly.

What we install

  • UniFi AI Pro & AI LPR cameras at the gate — 4K with on-device licence plate recognition. Every vehicle in, every vehicle out, logged with timestamp and an actual readable image of the plate.
  • UniFi AI 360s for the yard — panoramic ceiling-mount coverage for compound and material storage.
  • UniFi G5 Bullets on the perimeter — weatherproof, IK-rated, mounted high enough to survive site life.
  • A Dream Machine Pro and PoE switching in a lockable site cabinet — recording stays on site, on your kit. Footage doesn't sit on someone else's server.
  • 4G failover where there's no decent broadband — system keeps recording locally and syncs alerts over cellular until the WAN comes back.

What the site manager gets

  • A daily presence report — "vehicles on site between 07:30 and 17:00 today" — auto-emailed at the end of the working day.
  • Plate-watchlist alerts — flag specific subbie plates so you get a buzz when they arrive and a record of when they left.
  • Searchable history — "show me every visit by plate AB12 CDE last month" returns clips and timestamps in seconds.
  • Out-of-hours alerts only — the system stays quiet during normal working hours and only buzzes for movement on the gate after the site is closed.
  • One dashboard, on a phone — iOS and Android, two-factor, no public ports opened on the router.

The legal bit, handled

Workplace CCTV with ANPR has real obligations — ICO registration, a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA), appropriate signage at every entrance, a lawful basis for processing, and a retention policy you can actually point at. We bake the paperwork into the install package so you don't have to figure it out: we draft the DPIA, supply the signs, and hand over a one-page "what we record, how long for, who can see it" sheet your H&S team can file. No legal advice beyond what we hand you in writing — if you want a solicitor to bless it, we'll happily co-operate.

For homes & families

Peace of mind, not surveillance.

Check the workshop. Watch the dog while you're at the office. Make sure the gardener turned up on the day they said. Get useful alerts when the kids are home from school. None of it needs to live on Ring's, Nest's or Hik's servers.

Pets & the back of the house

  • Animal detection on AI-capable cameras — the system tells the difference between the dog moving and a person at the back gate, so the alerts you get on holiday are the ones that matter.
  • Two-way audio on indoor units — tell the dog off when it's on the sofa, reassure it during fireworks, talk to the cat sitter through the kitchen camera.
  • Clip-on-event recording — the system only keeps clips when something happened, so a fortnight away doesn't fill the recorder with empty hallway footage.

Family check-ins (the careful version)

A camera on a child's room or a relative's hallway is never a casual decision. We treat these installs like the considered job they are: an explicit conversation about what the camera is and isn't for, a fixed schedule (e.g. "off entirely during the day", "on only when the door is opened"), masked zones for private areas, and an audit trail of every login. For households where someone with special needs or reduced mobility lives, we'll happily build a system to give carers and family peace of mind — but we'll be straight with you up front: a camera is not a fall-alarm and not a medical device. Pair it with dedicated telecare (a Lifeline pendant or equivalent) for the medical-emergency side of things, and the camera does what it's good at: showing you, calmly, that everything is fine.

Privacy by design

Schedules, geofences and footage that stays where it belongs.

Most CCTV is "on, all the time, watching everything". That's rarely what you actually want, and increasingly it's not what's legal either. Every Luma install ships with the controls below configured from day one — not as a paid add-on, not as a thing you discover six months in.

What we configure as standard

  • Recording schedules — cameras can be off during occupied hours and on overnight, off during business hours and on at the weekend, or any other pattern you actually want. Schedules can vary by camera; the office camera doesn't have to follow the same rules as the back-garden one.
  • Geofenced arming — when the last household phone leaves a configured radius around the property, the internal cameras switch on; when the first phone is back, they switch off. No "did I arm the alarm?".
  • Masked / privacy zones — the camera that watches the drive doesn't need to also be looking through the neighbour's bedroom window. We mask sensitive zones at the camera, so they aren't recorded in the first place.
  • On-site recording — footage lives on a small NVR or Cloud Key in your house or office. Encrypted off-site backup is available if you want it, but it's a deliberate choice, not the default.
  • No public ports — remote viewing goes through an encrypted relay or your own VPN. Nothing on your router is opened to the internet for the cameras to work.
  • Per-user access — each viewer has their own login, two-factor on every account, an audit trail of who watched what.

What we'll never do

  • Pipe your footage to a third-party cloud service "for analysis".
  • Install a system that stops working when its subscription lapses.
  • Open a port on your firewall so you can watch the cameras from your phone.
  • Hand you a system you can't audit, change or take with you if you ever stop working with us.
