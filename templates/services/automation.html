Services/ Home Automation
Local-first smart home

Smart homes that are private and stay running.

We build smart homes around Home Assistant — local, open-source, fast, and not at the mercy of a vendor changing the API or switching the lights off when their server has a bad afternoon.

Why local-first matters

Most "smart home" platforms send every light switch through someone else's cloud. That's slow, fragile, and a privacy choice you didn't consciously make. With Home Assistant the brain lives in your house — your scenes work even if your internet's out, your data doesn't leak, and you can change anything without asking permission.

What we automate

  • Lighting — circadian-tuned, scene-controlled, motion-aware. Hue, Lutron, Shelly or in-wall Zigbee modules.
  • Climate — thermostats, underfloor zones, and per-room heating logic that actually saves you money.
  • Security — UniFi Protect cameras, door & window sensors, "leaving the house" routines that arm everything.
  • Energy — solar / battery / EV monitoring, plus automations that shift loads to cheap-rate windows.
  • Audio & media — Sonos, Apple TV, room-by-room control via dashboards and voice.
  • Access — smart locks, intercom integrations, time-bounded access codes for trades.

Equipment we trust

The brain

Home Assistant on a dedicated mini-PC or Home Assistant Yellow, with off-site encrypted backups. The thing that runs your house should not be a Raspberry Pi on a sad SD card.

Radios

Zigbee2MQTT for sensors and lighting, Z-Wave for door/window kit, Matter where it makes sense. We'll mix protocols when it's the right call, not for the sake of it.

Dashboards

Wall tablets, voice (Apple Home / Alexa / Google), or an Android tablet running a custom Home Assistant dashboard. Whatever fits how you actually live.

Network

Smart home and IoT live on their own VLAN, segregated from your computers. Done right, you get Wi-Fi reliability your IoT vendors did not promise.

We run it ourselves

We deliberately deploy every product we sell. Marco's own home runs the full UniFi + Home Assistant + Zigbee2MQTT stack — the same kit we install for clients. If something isn't reliable enough for our daily life, it doesn't make it onto your quote.
