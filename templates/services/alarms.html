Why false alarms matter

An alarm that cries wolf gets switched off, and an alarm that is switched off protects nothing. Most of the work in a good install is in sensor placement and in pairing every trigger with a camera view, so the notification carries enough information to act on. That is also why we arm on geofence rather than on a keypad routine nobody keeps up — the system does the remembering.

Alongside the cameras, not instead of them

An alarm tells you something happened. A camera tells you what. Installed together on one dashboard they answer both questions in one place — see CCTV installation for how we design the camera side, and access control if entry credentials are part of the picture.
