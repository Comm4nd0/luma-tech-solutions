CCTV & video surveillance

We design and install UniFi Protect CCTV — a local-recording system with no monthly cloud subscription. Footage stays on your kit, and you keep full control.

Cameras we typically install

  • UniFi G5 Bullet — the workhorse exterior camera. Discreet, weatherproof, sharp.
  • UniFi AI Pro — 4K with on-device person, vehicle and package detection. Fewer false alerts, smarter notifications.

  • Drone-planned camera placement — coverage angles verified from above before a single hole is drilled.

Access control

For businesses and shared sites we install proper access control — door readers, phone-based unlocks and tap-to-enter cards, all on the same dashboard as your network and CCTV.

  • Gate intercoms — video doorbells or commercial intercoms with two-way audio and remote unlock.
  • Visitor management — temporary access codes for tradespeople and Airbnb guests, with a photo at entry and a record of who came in when.
  • Time-based rules — the cleaner gets 8–10am Monday only, automatically.
  • Phone-based keys — your phone unlocks the door; fobs as backup. Revoke instantly when staff leave.

Intruder alarms

Ring Alarm Pro for residential, commercial-grade panels for businesses. Wired into the same Home Assistant or UniFi dashboard your CCTV runs on, so events flow together.

  • Door / window contacts, motion sensors, glass-break sensors.
  • Auto-arm on leave — when the last phone leaves the geofence, the system arms itself.
  • Notifications with snapshots — you get a camera still at the moment the alarm fires, so you know whether it's a fox or a foot.
  • Cellular backup keeps it working even if the internet is down.

Smart locks

Keyless entry done properly, integrated with the rest of the system rather than as a vendor island.

  • Keyless entry — phone, code, fingerprint or fob. No more "did I lock the door?".
  • Time-limited guest codes — give the dog-walker a code that only works 9–11am Monday-Friday.
  • Audit trail — see exactly who unlocked which door, when. Exportable.
  • Auto-lock on a timeout, optional auto-unlock on presence.

Cyber security

Most "smart home" break-ins don't come through a window — they come through a forgotten smart bulb or doorbell that shares your network with your laptop. We design networks that contain those risks from day one.

  • Separate networks for separate things — your smart devices, cameras, guest Wi-Fi and personal computers each live on their own private network. A compromised plug can't reach your photos.
  • Smart-device isolation — gadgets only talk out to the bits of the internet they actually need. Nothing else.
  • Active threat blocking — built-in protection at the front door blocks known-bad traffic before it reaches you.
  • Secure remote access — a private encrypted tunnel lets you reach things at home from anywhere, without opening any doors to the wider internet.
  • For businesses: every cable port authenticated — an unknown laptop plugged into an empty socket gets exactly nothing.

Drone-assisted security design

Cameras are easy to mount, hard to place. Before we drill, we fly an aerial drone survey of your property to map roof lines, tree cover, dead angles and approach routes — so cameras are positioned to actually see what matters and not just the back of a hedge.

  • Coverage zones planned from above with line-of-sight verified.
  • Blind spots identified before the install, not after.
  • Cable runs pre-planned, so install day is shorter and tidier.
  • Aerial imagery handed over with the runbook.
