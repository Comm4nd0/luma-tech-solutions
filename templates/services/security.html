Cyber security

Most "smart home" break-ins don't come through a window — they come through a forgotten smart bulb or doorbell that shares your network with your laptop. We design networks that contain those risks from day one.

  • Separate networks for separate things — your smart devices, cameras, guest Wi-Fi and personal computers each live on their own private network. A compromised plug can't reach your photos.
  • Smart-device isolation — gadgets only talk out to the bits of the internet they actually need. Nothing else.
  • Active threat blocking — built-in protection at the front door blocks known-bad traffic before it reaches you.
  • Secure remote access — a private encrypted tunnel lets you reach things at home from anywhere, without opening any doors to the wider internet.
  • For businesses: every cable port authenticated — an unknown laptop plugged into an empty socket gets exactly nothing.

The segmentation that does this is part of the network design, not an add-on afterwards — see how we build the network.
