We design and install UniFi Protect CCTV — a local-recording system with no monthly cloud subscription. Footage stays on your kit, and you keep full control.

Cameras we typically install

  • UniFi G5 Bullet — the workhorse exterior camera. Discreet, weatherproof, sharp.
  • UniFi AI Pro — 4K with on-device person, vehicle and package detection. Fewer false alerts, smarter notifications.
  • UniFi AI 360 — panoramic ceiling-mount for forecourts, large rooms or open courtyards.
  • UniFi G4 Instant — small, magnetic interior camera for blind-spots, garages or workshops.

What you get

  • Carefully planned camera placement — coverage angles checked on site before a single hole is drilled.
  • A recorder that lives in your house — small for homes, rack-mount for larger sites. Footage stays with you.
  • Smart detection — the cameras tell person from vehicle from delivery, on the camera itself, so notifications mean something.
  • View from anywhere — secure phone, tablet or web access. Your footage never goes through a third-party cloud.

On-device AI, no cloud needed

The newer UniFi cameras run their detection AI on the camera itself — there's a small neural processor inside each unit. That means notifications are smart from day one, your footage never leaves your network so an AI can "look at it", and there's no monthly cloud bill paying for it.

  • Person, vehicle, package — the most useful classes, on every AI-capable camera. So the doorbell stops alerting on every leaf that blows past.
  • Number plate recognition on AI Pro and AI LPR units — log every vehicle that comes up the drive, with the plate searchable later.
  • Smart Detection Search — "show me every person who came through the gate yesterday between 4 and 6pm" returns a few clips, not three hours of footage.
  • Face recognition (optional, opt-in) — the system learns the people who live there and stops alerting on them, so the alerts that do come through are meaningful.
  • Animal detection — useful if you'd rather not get woken up because the fox is back.

If number-plate capture, scheduled recording or geofenced arming is the reason you're here, that has its own page: AI camera systems and ANPR.

Camera placement, done right

Cameras are easy to mount, hard to place. Before we drill, we walk the property, sketch coverage zones, check sightlines and identify blind spots — so cameras are positioned to actually see what matters and not just the back of a hedge.

  • Coverage zones planned with line-of-sight verified room-by-room.
  • Blind spots identified before the install, not after.
  • Cable runs pre-planned, so install day is shorter and tidier.
  • A written placement plan handed over with the runbook.
