Camera placement comes first

We walk the property with you to identify the entrances and areas that matter, check sightlines and plan cable routes. We discuss how visible the equipment should be and agree the positions before drilling.

  • Coverage of the driveway, doors, garden and outbuildings as required.
  • Camera choice matched to distance, lighting and the detail you need to see.
  • Privacy zones and recording settings discussed before handover.
  • A written plan and a clear explanation of any remaining blind spots.

Recordings stored at home

We use UniFi Protect with a recorder at your property. The core local recording system does not require a monthly cloud-storage subscription. Recording can continue during a broadband outage while the cameras, network and recorder have power; remote viewing needs an internet connection.

Useful alerts, chosen for your needs

Compatible cameras and recording equipment can identify events such as people or vehicles. The available detection and search features depend on the equipment and software, so your quote specifies the features being supplied.

For specialist requirements such as number-plate capture, see our AI cameras and ANPR service.

A handover you can follow

We set up access for the people you choose and show you how to view live cameras, find an event and export a clip. You'll know where recordings are stored, how long they are retained and who has access.

Read our approach to camera privacy for more about placement and privacy settings.

Support when you need it

Every installation includes 30 days of post-install support. An optional care plan can cover ongoing monitoring and maintenance. If you're calling about an existing fault, we agree the diagnostic visit fee and scope before booking.
