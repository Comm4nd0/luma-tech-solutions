Wi-Fi & Networking, done properly.

We design and install enterprise-grade networks using the Ubiquiti UniFi stack — the same kit running in offices, hotels and stadiums, sized sensibly for homes and small businesses.

The UniFi stack

UniFi gives you a single dashboard for Wi-Fi, switching, routing and (optionally) CCTV. It's reliable, repairable, and you actually own your network — no monthly cloud subscription holding it hostage.

What we typically install

  • UniFi Dream Machine / Cloud Gateway — your router, firewall and controller, all in one box.
  • UniFi Access Points — Wi-Fi 6 / 6E units, properly placed using a heat-map survey, not just stuck near the router.
  • UniFi Switches — managed PoE switches so you can power cameras and APs cleanly over Ethernet.
  • Structured cabling — Cat6 runs to every AP and camera location, terminated to a patch panel in the comms cupboard.
  • UniFi Protect (optional) — local-recording CCTV with no cloud subscription. Footage stays on your kit.
