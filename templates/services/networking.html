When your current Wi-Fi isn't enough

Mesh systems can work well in a suitable layout. In a home with thick walls, several floors or separate buildings, wireless links between units can struggle. Adding another box without checking the signal path may leave the same rooms uncovered.

There is no single floor-area threshold or access-point count that fits every property. We look at the building, its existing wiring and what you need to connect.

What we check on the survey

  • Where calls drop, streaming stalls or devices lose their connection.
  • Whether the limitation is your broadband, Wi-Fi coverage or existing equipment.
  • Wall construction, available cable routes and discreet mounting positions.
  • Garden offices, annexes and outdoor areas that need coverage.

A network with a clear design

We normally use wired UniFi access points where cable routes are practical. Each has its own connection back to the network, avoiding dependence on a wireless link between access points. Where cabling isn't practical, we explain the alternatives and their limits.

  • Access points in the right places — selected for the rooms and outdoor areas they need to cover.
  • Planned cable runs — with routes and the finish agreed before drilling.
  • Power over Ethernet — compatible access points and cameras get power and data through one cable.
  • Separate guest and smart-device networks — configured where useful for your household.
  • Diagnostics and documentation — so the system can be understood and maintained.

A tidy finish and a usable handover

We discuss equipment locations and cable routes with you, label the installation and provide your admin access and a network diagram. Where a standard bracket doesn't fit, we can design and 3D print a suitable mount.

Every installation includes 30 days of post-install support. We can also include an optional home care plan in the proposal.

What your quote covers

  • Configuration, testing and handover.
