The story

Marco Baldanza founded {{ SITE_NAME }} after a decade engineering infrastructure and software for businesses where things have to actually work. The pattern at home was always the same: friends and neighbours juggling three different "specialists" — a Wi-Fi guy, a web guy, an alarm guy — none of whom owned the whole picture.

So we built a company that does. We design the network, build the software, automate the home, and stick around to support it.

About Marco

Marco served in the Royal Marines Information Systems (RMIS) branch, building and maintaining mission-critical networks and communications in environments where failure wasn't an option. He founded {{ SITE_NAME }} to bring that same standard of reliability to homes and businesses in {{ SITE_REGION }}.

Today he runs a full smart home and UniFi network at home, flies an aerial drone for site surveys, builds custom software, and — when he's not laying Cat6 in someone's loft — is probably re-arranging YAML in Home Assistant.
